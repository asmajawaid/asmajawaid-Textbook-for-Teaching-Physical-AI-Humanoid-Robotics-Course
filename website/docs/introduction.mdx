# Introduction: The Dawn of Physical AI

Welcome to the forefront of artificial intelligence and robotics. This course embarks on an exciting journey into the world of **Physical AI**, a groundbreaking field where intelligent agents begin to perceive, interact with, and learn from the physical world, much like we do. We stand at a unique intersection in technological history, witnessing the convergence of three powerful domains: **Humanoid Robotics**, advanced **AI Perception**, and the reasoning capabilities of **Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

The future of AI extends beyond digital spaces into the physical world. This capstone course introduces Physical AI—AI systems that function in reality and comprehend physical laws. Students learn to design, simulate, and deploy humanoid robots capable of natural human interactions using ROS 2, Gazebo, and NVIDIA Isaac.

## Why Physical AI Matters

Humanoid robots are poised to excel in our human-centered world because they share our physical form and can be trained with abundant data from interacting in human environments. This represents a significant transition from AI models confined to digital environments to embodied intelligence that operates in physical space. Our goal is to bridge the gap between the digital brain and the physical body, applying AI knowledge to control Humanoid Robots in simulated and real-world environments.

## Learning Outcomes

By the end of this course, you will be able to:

-   Understand Physical AI principles and the concept of embodied intelligence.
-   Master ROS 2 (Robot Operating System) for advanced robotic control.
-   Simulate complex robotic systems and environments with Gazebo and Unity.
-   Develop sophisticated AI applications for robotics using the NVIDIA Isaac platform.
-   Design and control humanoid robots for natural and effective human-robot interaction.
-   Integrate GPT-based models to create conversational and cognitive robotics.

## Weekly Breakdown

This course is structured over 13 weeks, taking you from foundational concepts to building a complete autonomous system.

-   **Weeks 1-2: Introduction to Physical AI**
    -   Foundations of Physical AI and embodied intelligence.
    -   From digital AI to robots that understand physical laws.
    -   Overview of the humanoid robotics landscape.
    -   Sensor systems: LIDAR, cameras, IMUs, force/torque sensors.

-   **Weeks 3-5: ROS 2 Fundamentals**
    -   ROS 2 architecture and core concepts.
    -   Nodes, topics, services, and actions.
    -   Building ROS 2 packages with Python.
    -   Launch files and parameter management.

-   **Weeks 6-7: Robot Simulation with Gazebo**
    -   Gazebo simulation environment setup.
    *   URDF and SDF robot description formats.
    -   Physics simulation and sensor simulation.
    -   Introduction to Unity for robot visualization.

-   **Weeks 8-10: NVIDIA Isaac Platform**
    -   NVIDIA Isaac SDK and Isaac Sim.
    -   AI-powered perception and manipulation.
    -   Reinforcement learning for robot control.
    -   Sim-to-real transfer techniques.

-   **Weeks 11-12: Humanoid Robot Development**
    -   Humanoid robot kinematics and dynamics.
    -   Bipedal locomotion and balance control.
    -   Manipulation and grasping with humanoid hands.
    -   Natural human-robot interaction design.

-   **Week 13: Conversational Robotics**
    -   Integrating GPT models for conversational AI in robots.
    -   Speech recognition and natural language understanding.
    -   Multi-modal interaction: speech, gesture, and vision.

## Assessments

-   ROS 2 package development project
-   Gazebo simulation implementation
-   Isaac-based perception pipeline
-   Capstone: Simulated humanoid robot with conversational AI
